        HOW TO PLAY

        October 1996. Kingsgate Mall closes for good next week, and Gatehouse Security has put you on the night shift: ten at night to six in the morning. The last night guard, Dale Mercer, stopped coming in on Tuesday. His car is still in the lot.

        Lock everything. Pull the shop gates down and padlock them, lock the outside doors. The alarm panel in the security office (and your map) shows what's open. Things you locked won't always stay locked. Walk the rounds: turn the key in all six clock stations, every round.

        Find out what's happening. Read Dale's logbook. Look at what he left behind. Your notes fill in as you go, and at six you'll file an incident report: get it right.

        The mannequins only move when nobody can see them. Not in anyone's view, not in a flashlight beam, not on the camera someone in the office is watching. In the dark you can't see them, so they can move. A locked gate stops them. An unlocked one doesn't. Don't all look away at once.

        From one in the morning, something in Dale's uniform walks his round with his keys. If it sees you it comes for you. Run, break its line of sight, hide, or shine your flashlight full in its face.
