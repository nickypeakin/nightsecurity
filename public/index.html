    HOW TO PLAY

    November 1996. Kingsgate Mall is a fenced-off pile of rubble, and the night shift that used to guard it is out of work. The city's still awake, and some of the people who run it have jobs for somebody who doesn't scare easily.

    Take jobs. Walk into the big letter on the radar (and the glowing ring on the street) to start one. Ray Okafor sells used cars; Lena Voss runs the harbour; Dale Mercer, the old night guard, has one last thing for you.

    Steal cars. Walk up to any car and press F, even one with somebody in it. Stars mean the police want you: get out of their sight until they give up, or drive into Spray-N-Go. If they hold on to you, you're busted.

    Buy guns at Kingsgate Arms on the Strip. Thirteen of Kingsgate Mall's old security tapes are lost round the city. Walk into your place (⌂) to save.
