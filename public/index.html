    HOW TO PLAY

    November 1996. Kingsgate Mall is a fenced-off pile of rubble, and you used to guard it. Now you're at home in Kingsgate Heights with a car out front, and some of the people who run this city have work for somebody who doesn't scare easily.

    Jobs. The big letters on the radar (O, V, D) are Ray Okafor, Lena Voss and Dale Mercer. Go to their door, walk in, and step into the ring in front of them to start a job.

    Buildings. Walk into a glowing door to go inside: your place (sleep in your bed to save), Kingsgate Arms for guns and armour, Sal's Diner for a meal when you're hurt, and the offices of the people with work.

    Cars. Your own car is parked by your place. Any other car is yours too if you press F next to it. Stars mean the police want you: get out of their sight until they give up, or drive into Spray-N-Go. Days pass, and the street lights come on at night.
